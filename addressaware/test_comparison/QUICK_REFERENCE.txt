================================================================================
KEY INSIGHT: Same Tokens, Different Embeddings
================================================================================

PalmTree Embedding:
    final = token_emb + seq_position_emb + segment_emb
            ↑           ↑                  ↑
            128-dim     128-dim            128-dim
            (frozen)    (frozen)           (frozen)

AddressAware Embedding:
    final = token_emb + seq_position_emb + segment_emb + address_emb
            ↑           ↑                  ↑             ↑
            128-dim     128-dim            128-dim       128-dim
            (frozen)    (frozen)           (frozen)      (TRAINABLE!)

The ONLY difference: AddressAware adds hierarchical address positional info!
